To me, systems are not software architectures—they are the cognitive and structural frameworks I design to optimize human execution.

As an avid learner, I try to view productivity and academic mastery as engineering problems that require deliberate design. This repository documents the exact behavioral frameworks that improve my memory, attention, and execution system.

What do I use for academics?

It is called blurting, and the idea is simple. Read your notes for 30 minutes, then write down everything you remember on paper. Reread to fill any memory gaps until you know the topic cold.

What do I use for productivity?

To be more productive with my time, I switched my phone from a colorful, dopamine-filled homescreen to a minimalist, dumb-phone setup. It has worked wonders for me ever since. Before this, I was plagued by the need to doom-scroll every waking minute of the day. After the change, however, I've slowly but surely started to do away with that habit. And it only takes around a day to get used to.

I also highly encourage you to use this setup as your daily-driver. Obviously, this isn't the only productive tool I use, but it is by far the most important.
