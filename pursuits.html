pursuits

interests & habits
Football ▶

I'm a football fan, or as some would say "soccer". I support Liverpool F.C. and watch their games in the context of tactical structure, analytics, and identity. The sport feels like a study in systems, decision-making, and collective tempo.

Under Fenway Sports Group, Liverpool famously became the pioneer of heavy data analytics in global sports. They treat football like a computer science problem—optimization, pattern recognition, and continuous adaptation under pressure.

Programming ▶

It’s probably obvious by now, but I love tinkering with technology and building new things from scratch. Honestly, the main reason I built this entire website was to turn my thoughts, interests, and side projects into a more deliberate digital home.

I’m planning to dive deeper into lower-level mechanics and data scripting next, expanding my toolkit into languages like C++, Python, and more advanced JavaScript patterns. I like the way software lets me turn abstract ideas into concrete systems.

Journaling ▶

I started journaling a few months ago, and honestly, it’s been incredibly refreshing to get my thoughts out of my head and onto a piece of paper. Whenever I have an idea, an emotion, or a weird feeling I don’t want to ignore, writing it down helps me make sense of it.

The habits of journaling and tracking my days became so engrained in my routine that I decided to take it to the next level: I created an entire program for it. I wanted a system that let me reflect consistently without friction, and it’s become one of the most useful tools in my life.

Reading ▶

I wouldn’t call this my biggest interest by any means, but I do like reading from time to time—whether it’s a regular book or the Bible. Honestly, it’s a great way to slow down and engage with ideas beyond the noise of the internet.

Whether I'm looking at a modern novel or diving into the historical texts and moral lessons of scripture, I like seeing how centuries-old writings can still teach us something about human nature, discipline, and purpose.
