I'm David Busha, a self-taught developer and high school junior based in Ethiopia. I engineered this site from scratch to serve as an independent digital sandbox—a clean personal home for hosting my original research papers, architectural web utilities, and continuous technical experiments.

My early work focuses on linguistic patterns, structural execution, and deep competitive systems. I currently spend my time running computational projects, tracking algorithmic models, and documenting my builds.

About Me

I was born in Toronto and moved to Ethiopia at age 6. I first jumped deep into structural linguistic arrays during my training for competitive exhibitions. This work culminated on the continental stage in Kampala, Uganda, where I placed 2nd overall in the Senior Category at the 6th Annual African Spelling Bee Finals, making history as the first podium placement representing Ethiopia.

Lately, I have pivoted those analytical frameworks toward software systems engineering and data synthesis, building out localized logic utilities while writing independent research components.
