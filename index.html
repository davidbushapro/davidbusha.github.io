Hello there! I'm David Busha, a self-taught 'developer' and high-school junior in Ethiopia. I programmed this page from scracth to serve as an independent digital sandbox-a clean personal home for hosting my research papers, genuine interests, and much more.

My interests lie at the intersection of computer science, language, and the humanities. I am particularly interested in how computational methods can help us understand language and patterns, and I am exploring these questions through independent research and technical projects. Beyond computing, I remain drawn to the social sciences-fields that shape the questions I ask and the way I approach problems.

ABOUT ME

I was born in Canada to Ethiopian parents and moved to Ethiopia at age 6. I first jumped deep into structural linguistic arrays during my training for competitive exhibitions. This work culminated on the continental stage in Kampala, Uganda, where I won silver in the Senior Category at the 6th Annual African Spelling Bee Finals, making history as the first podium placement representing Ethiopia.

Lately, I have pivoted those analytical frameworks toward software systems engineering and data synthesis, building out localized logic utilities while writing independent research and technical notes.
